* IMPORTANT WIRING RULE:
  - Both buttons use internal pull-up resistors (pinMode(..., INPUT_PULLUP)).
  - Connect one leg of each switch to the specified Nano pin (D11 or D12).
  - Connect the other leg directly to GND.
  - DO NOT connect either button to 5V!

SECTION 7: HARDWARE AUDIT & TROUBLESHOOTING CHECKLIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] 1. Check Button 1 Wiring: Is Button 1 wired between Pin D11 and GND? (NOT Pin A0, and NOT Pin 5V!)
[ ] 2. Check Button 2 Wiring: Is Button 2 wired between Pin D12 and GND?
[ ] 3. Check TB6612FNG STBY: Is the STBY pin tied directly to 5V? If STBY is open, motors will not spin!
[ ] 4. Check Motor Power VM: Is the LiPo battery connected and switched ON? USB alone cannot supply DC motors.
[ ] 5. Check Ground Unity: Are the Arduino GND, Motor Driver GND, Battery (-), and Buck GND all tied together?
[ ] 6. Check Decoupling Capacitor: Is a 470uF-1000uF capacitor soldered close to the TB6612FNG VM and GND pins?
[ ] 7. Check Sensor Clearance: Are the TCRT5000 sensors mounted 3mm to 5mm above the track surface?
