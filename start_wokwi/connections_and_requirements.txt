------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TABLE 4: SIMULATION HARDWARE & SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIMULATION ENVIRONMENT:
   - Platform: Wokwi Online Simulator (https://wokwi.com) or VS Code Wokwi Extension.
   - Target Board: Arduino Nano (ATmega328P, 16MHz).
   - Baud Rate: 115200 Baud (match in Serial Monitor window).

2. PHYSICAL ROBOT EQUIVALENTS:
   - Sensors: 8x TCRT5000 IR reflectance sensors or Pololu QTR-8A module.
   - Dual Motor Driver: TB6612FNG (recommended for low voltage drop) or L298N.
   - DC Motors: 2x N20 Micro Metal Gearmotors (6V, 500-1000 RPM).
   - Battery: 7.4V (2S) or 11.1V (3S) LiPo (450mAh - 850mAh).
   - Power Filtering: 470uF to 1000uF electrolytic capacitor across driver VM and GND.
   - Push Buttons: 2x 6x6mm momentary tactile switches.
